Our Rooms

Have a sneak peak into a world of comfort

Garden Suite

Inside, guests enjoy refined décor with natural textures, plush bedding, and thoughtful amenities, including a cozy lounge area and a well-appointed bathroom. Whether savoring morning coffee in the sunshine or unwinding with an evening stroll through winding garden paths, every moment here feels like a breath of fresh air.

Heritage Room

Step into timeless elegance with our Heritage Room, where history whispers through every detail. Nestled within the walls of a lovingly restored landmark, this room blends classic charm with modern comfort.

Willow Deluxe

Nestled in a serene setting, the Willow Deluxe Room offers a harmonious blend of modern sophistication and timeless charm. Step into a world where soft lighting,warm tones, and refined décor create an inviting atmosphere from the moment you arrive.

The Art of Relaxtion

Indulge in spaces and services that transform rest into an unforgettable experience.

Breakfast

Begin your day in the heart of nature, where the gentle warmth of the Mediterranean sun kisses your skin and a soft breeze carries the scent of blooming herbs. Our Garden Buffet is nestled among lush greenery, vibrant flowers, and whispering olive trees, creating a serene oasis for your morning meal.

Wellness & Spa

Here, guests can indulge in rejuvenating treatments inspired by Mediterranean traditions: olive oil massages, sea salt scrubs, and herbal steam rituals. The tranquil thermal suite features a heated pool, aromatic sauna, and a quiet relaxation lounge where herbal teas and fresh fruit await.

The Courtyard Pool

Step into a serene, Mediterranean‑style oasis at the Courthouse Pool — a refreshing, crystal‑clear basin encircled by warm, textured natural stone and softened by abundant greenery. The design draws on the coastal charm of the Mediterranean, with sun‑bleached stone arches, olive‑tree‑like foliage, and carefully placed shade sails to create a tranquil, resort‑like atmosphere
